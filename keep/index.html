KEEP

Sir Julian the Brave stands at the gate.

Three sieges on winding roads. Three heroes fight on the map — not portraits in a drawer. Build, rally, and hold the stone keep.

  • Sir Julian the Brave — great hero, black & blue, sword and Lionheart dash.
  • Shadow Aussie — light archer. Nightfall is a rapid bow volley.
  • Papa — warm guardian. Bulwark stun and a lane-blocking wall.

THE WATCH

Hold the winding road.

  1. Tap an empty pad to raise a tower. Each has two upgrades with a new trick.
  2. Tap a hero or their portrait, then tap the path to send them. They fight on their own.
  3. Barracks soldiers (and Papa’s wall) body-block ground foes. Nightwings fly over.
  4. Call the next wave early for a little gold. Three maps, each harder. The last wave of Night Gate is the boss.

There are a few warm secrets in the grass and on the banners. Nothing mean. Nothing biographical.
