The Last Defense of Camelot

Three knights hold the southern road.

After the wars that forged the Round Table, a new shadow rises. Arthur and the greater knights ride north. Julian, Austin, and Papa hold the approaches while Morgan’s thorns creep toward the gate.

  • Julian the Lionhearted — youngest, scarlet and gold, longsword. Mobile melee. Lionheart dash and smash.
  • Austin the Shadow — true tank. Darkened plate, hammer like a fist. Enemies halt to fight him. Bulwark slam and a lane wall.
  • Papa of the Longbow — veteran, green hood, yew bow. Long range, priority elites. Volley rains stout shafts.

THE SOUTHERN WATCH

Hold the road to Camelot.

  1. Tap an empty pad to raise a tower. Each has two upgrades with a new trick.
  2. Tap a hero or their portrait, then tap the path to send them. They fight on their own. Tap again to deselect.
  3. Barracks soldiers (and Austin’s wall) body-block ground foes. Morgan’s Hounds slip past some blockers.
  4. Call the next wave early for a little gold. Seven maps, each more blighted. A Thorn-Drake and a shade of Mordred are bosses — if a boss leaks, the keep falls at once.
  5. Title picks Standard (the siege you know, stepping up) or Veteran (thicker hides). The choice lasts the whole run.

There are a few warm secrets in the grass and on the banners. Nothing mean. Nothing biographical.
